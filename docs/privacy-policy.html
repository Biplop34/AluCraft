Privacy Policy

This page explains how AluCraft handles information used in its Android app and Windows desktop app, including AluCraft, Inch Calculator, Thai Fabrication, customer and work-history tools, Billing, Price List, and Invoice Generator.

In brief: Customer, fabrication, billing, invoice, and shop-profile data is stored locally on your Android device or Windows user profile. Local-file backup is available, and Google Drive backup is optional and requires your authorization.
01

Information you may enter

Depending on the features you use, AluCraft may store customer names, phone numbers and site locations; invoice customer names, addresses and optional phone numbers; shop name, address, phone numbers and logo; measurement photos you choose for Smart Scanner; aluminium and glass selections; measurements and calculation results; work-history and customer records; square-feet and fanlight rates; invoice descriptions, quantities (including feet and inch marks), rates and totals; and measurement or base-eight conversion history. Enter only information you are authorized to store. The Price List page displays built-in aluminium profile price references and does not need customer information.

02

Storage on your device

Customer records, job measurements, calculation results, billing records, generated invoice records, and invoice shop details are stored locally by the Android app on your device or by the Windows desktop app in your Windows user profile. The Windows app uses a persistent WebView2 app-data folder under %LOCALAPPDATA%\AluCraft. Saved invoices include their customer details, item descriptions, quantities, rates, totals, language, and signature choice. Quantity entries may include feet and inch marks; the marks are ignored when calculating item totals. Shop details may include an uploaded logo. Saved billing records can include the rates associated with a fabrication job and the information needed to display its bill breakdown.

When you save an invoice or billing report, AluCraft creates an image or PDF on your device. Invoice images and PDFs are saved in a shop-specific folder under Downloads/AluCraft, named after the shop and followed by “Invoice”. Billing and other report exports are saved in Downloads/AluCraft. On older Android versions, files may use AluCraft's app-specific Downloads storage. Exported files are separate from the records saved inside the app and can remain after an in-app record is deleted. Sharing is started only when you tap Share and choose an available platform destination.

03

Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional. If you connect a Google account and grant the requested Drive app-data permission, the Android or Windows app can save and restore a backup in that account's private appDataFolder. Both apps use the same supported backup filename and data format, allowing supported data to be transferred between Android and Windows with the same Google account. This is an app-private area, not a general public folder in Drive. The Windows app requires a Desktop OAuth client configured for the same Google Cloud project.

The app's backup package includes customer and work records, calculator state, measurement history, and a dump of supported app storage. This can include saved Invoice Generator records and the invoice shop profile, as well as customer information, measurements, rates, and totals. Local JSON backups contain supported app data too. Treat backup files as private and review a backup before sharing or restoring it.

Google processes account authentication and Drive storage under its own terms and privacy policy. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

You choose whether to connect an account and use backup or restore. You can revoke the app's Google access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized Drive operations but does not necessarily delete a backup already stored in Drive.

04

How information is used

Information is used to scan measurement photos, calculate fabrication measurements and bills, create and display invoices, manage customer and work records, save invoice shop details, produce requested exports, and perform backup or restore when you request or enable those features.

05

Sharing and disclosure

When you use Google Drive backup, supported backup data is sent to the Google account you authorize. For Smart Scanner, if a Gemini API key is configured, the selected measurement photo is sent to Google's Generative Language API for recognition; if no key is configured or the request cannot be completed, the app falls back to on-device ML Kit OCR. Google processes data sent to its API under its applicable terms and privacy policy. When you tap Share, the selected image or PDF is offered through the sharing options available on Android or Windows to a destination you choose. AluCraft does not automatically publish customer or invoice details, and we do not sell personal information. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, exported files, or Google account may be able to see information available there.

06

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you delete the relevant customer, job, billing, or invoice record, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Invoice images, PDFs, and report files saved to Downloads may remain until you delete them separately. A local JSON backup or Google Drive backup may also remain until you delete that file or backup. Deleting an in-app record does not automatically delete exported files or earlier backup copies.

07

Security

The Android app relies on Android application-storage protections. The Windows app stores its files in the signed-in Windows user profile and protects its saved Google authorization with Windows user-level encryption. Google account authorization and Drive security features also apply. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device and Google account.

08

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose fabrication calculator and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the project maintainer using the support link below.

09

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data handling change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.

10

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub project support page.
